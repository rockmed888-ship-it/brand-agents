How it works

Whichever device you are on, the other one has hands.

You talk to Grok. Dale moves the Windows PC. DD moves the Android phone. Same Grok account on the phone app or the computer.

01

Install both

Dale on Windows. DD on Android. Leave them running.

02

Paste one URL into Grok

Connectors → Custom. Works in the Grok phone app and on the computer.

03

Talk from wherever you are

Phone: “Dale, do this on the PC.” Computer: “DD, do this on the phone.”

Autonomy model

Default is the most conservative setting appropriate for first run.

Suggest

Proposes next steps. Nothing runs until you approve.

Confirm each action

Plans may be multi-step; each click or keystroke still needs confirmation.

Limited autonomy

Short, scoped sequences you pre-approve can complete without nagging on every micro-step.

Higher autonomy

Longer work within caps you set. Kill switch and action log remain on.

Build Trust Tutorial

First run includes a short guided path: give a command, review a proposal, approve or deny, practice pause/stop, open the action log. It is not optional theater—it is how the product teaches control.
