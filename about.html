About Brand Agents

Brand Agents builds click pets that live on your phone or computer and work under the control levels you set. Grok is the brain.

Brand Agents is the business name and the connector name. We are a software company focused on permissioned clicks— not a foundation-model lab.

The campaign is Join the Click. They click. You stay on Send.

How we describe ourselves

“Brand Agents is a software company building named click pets — Patch and BeeDee — that live on your phone or computer and work under the control levels you set. Grok is the brain.”

What we ship

  • Patch and BeeDee — click pets on Android and computer
  • Grok connector named Brand Agents — one Server URL
  • Custom pets after you connect: describe someone, name them, Grok builds it
  • Progressive trust, permissions, and workflow memory
  • BYOK or managed AI access via official provider APIs

What we are not

  • Not a claim to train frontier foundation models
  • Not “just a chatbot with a face”
  • Not a third junk 3D man on the storefront — starters are Patch and BeeDee
