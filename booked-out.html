Booked Out is the social upgrade. We build the business social page, then keep the connections, the posts, and the presence up. You win when people can still find you.

What this install does

It makes a Booked Out folder on the computer with a one-page social card: who you are, what you do, and how to reach you. That page is the presence. Brand Agents, if you add them later, are the hands that follow up. Brain Connector, if you add it later, is the brain on the site. This file is only Booked Out.

  1. Download booked-out.ps1.
  2. Run it in PowerShell. It asks for the business name.
  3. Open the page it writes. That is the social card.
