Trust & control

The agent never outruns the control you set.

Brand Agents act under your direction and autonomy settings. Permissions are explicit. Autonomy starts conservative. You can stop everything instantly.

An agent with mouse and keyboard control can make mistakes. You accept that risk when you grant input permission and raise autonomy. Review the risk disclaimer before enabling control.

Autonomy ladder

Default on first run: Observe only (or Suggest only if you opt in during setup).

1 · Observe

Watches only after capture permission. No clicks, no typing. Reports what it sees.

2 · Suggest

Proposes next actions. You approve every step before anything runs.

3 · Confirm each action

May prepare multi-step plans; each click/type still needs your confirmation.

4 · Limited autonomy

Can complete short, scoped sequences you pre-approve (e.g. “save this file”).

5 · Higher autonomy

Longer multi-step work within caps you set. Kill switch and log still always on.

Build Trust Tutorial

A guided, interactive sequence—impossible to skip on first run—that teaches safe expansion of capability.

Step A

Give a simple command

Example: “What is on my screen?” at Observe level.

Step B

Review a suggestion

See a proposed click or keystroke described in plain language before it happens.

Step C

Approve or deny

Practice the confirmation gate. Denial is always one click.

Step D

Raise one level

Only when ready—move from Suggest to Confirm, never jump to full autonomy by default.

Step E

Use the kill switch

Global pause / emergency stop returns control to you immediately.

Step F

Open the action log

Every decision and action is reviewable. Trust is earned and auditable.
