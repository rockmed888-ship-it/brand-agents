Questions people actually ask

Business pricing & demo

Can Patch or BeeDee move my mouse and type?

Only after you grant input permission, and only within the autonomy level you set. You can revoke that permission anytime. Default setup is conservative.

What happens if I run out of managed capacity?

Managed AI stops acting and shows upgrade or top-up options. Your kill switch and local presence still work. BYOK users continue against their own provider limits.

Do you train public models on my screen?

Screen content is processed for the agent to function. It is not stored long-term on our servers by default and is not used to train public foundation models unless you explicitly opt in. See Privacy.

What if the agent makes a mistake?

Agents can mis-click or mistype. You accept that risk when you enable control. Use Suggest / Confirm levels until you are comfortable. Full risk disclosure is in Legal.

Windows or Mac?

Windows is first. macOS is next. Requirements are listed on Download.
